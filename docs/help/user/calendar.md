# Google Calendar

Preferences → **Google Calendar** connects one Google account and one calendar. The same setting is on the website and in the Android app.

**Connect Google account** opens Google so you can choose the account. After that, pick the calendar (only calendars you can edit are listed) and save it. Until a calendar is selected, nothing is written.

Create a calendar in Google that is only for TV Tracker, and pick that one. These meetings then stay off your personal calendar. In Google Calendar: **Create calendar**, name it (for example TV Tracker), then select it here.

## What gets a meeting

A meeting is created when TV Tracker knows a date **and** the title is on **Wishlist** or a list you marked **Auto-select**. A title that is only on some other personal list does not get a meeting.

| When | Meeting |
|------|---------|
| Movie release date is in the future, or still inside the 30-day theater window | All-day **In theaters** on that date |
| Movie later appears on a streaming service you checked in Preferences | All-day **Streaming** meeting on the day it appears. A release that is still in the future uses that release date. Movies already on your services the first time they are seen do not get a meeting |
| Show episode has an air date today or later, and the show is on one of your streaming services | All-day meeting for that episode |

Episode dates are the ones the app has already loaded from Trakt (about the next month, plus any later dates opened on My calendar). A show that is not on any of your streaming services does not get episode meetings.

Saving that title’s lists creates the meeting when the date is already known and still in the future. A finished title does not call Google, and other titles are left alone. If a date shows up later, save that title’s lists again to write the meeting.

If Google refuses the saved login, the website sends you to approve calendar access again and keeps the calendar you already picked. Picking the calendar, or changing the reminder, updates every meeting.

## Reminder

Every meeting uses one notification: **Popup**, **Email**, or **None**. The default is a popup **1 day** before. Change the number and choose minutes, hours, or days (up to 28 days), then **Save reminder**. That updates meetings already on the calendar.

Meetings are all-day, so the time is before midnight at the start of that day. 1 day before is midnight on the day before.

## What the meeting says

The title is the movie or the show plus season and episode. The description includes the overview, year, runtime, genres, network, cast (actor and character; favorite actors are marked), which of your services have it, and a link back to the title in TV Tracker.

## Disconnect

**Disconnect** stops new meetings and removes upcoming ones this app created. Meetings that already happened stay on the calendar.
