# Notifications

Same as [Alerts](release_alerts) — open **Alerts** in the nav.

Active rows are brighter; **new** (unacked) rows have a thick gold frame and a gold title bar. Dimmed rows were dismissed. **Inbox** shows only new alerts as single rows. **Ack** drops the gold highlight without hiding the card; **Unack** puts it back in Inbox. **Dismiss** / **Restore** hide or bring back an alert. **Hide dismissed** (default) keeps the list to active only — use **Show dismissed** when you want history. **Newest first** / **Oldest first** sorts by time; **Pin** a show or movie to keep its alerts on top; **Grouped by show** collapses a show’s episode alerts until you expand them. **Dismiss all** hides every active alert; **Ack all** only clears new highlights. Alerts also auto-dismiss when you **Watch** the episode/movie in this app (that updates the shared progress/watched cache immediately), or on the next alert refresh once the local cache already shows it watched.
